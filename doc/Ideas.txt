
Improve trouble score:
======================
1st trouble score implementation in nmesh works like this:
----------------------------------------------------------
*do a full RK step
*check for trouble
*if DG and trouble, redo entire RK step in all elements

2nd implementation is like this:
--------------------------------
*get r = RHS(w) for a RK substep
*compute new substep u (e.g. u += r dt/6)
*check u for trouble
*if DG and trouble:
  undo addition of r to u (e.g. u -= r dt/6)
  recompute r = RHS(w) with FV
  recompute new u (e.g. u += r dt/6)

+All is local. We never need surface communication to decide if there is
 trouble!!!
+We can take substep back locally and never need to go back for all elements.

About artificial viscosity (av):
================================
*1st implementatation in nmesh adds  mu*d_x^2 u  to RHS
*mu is O(h) and thus this converges to zero with 1st order

*maybe we could simply use Kreiss-Oliger dissipation, i.e. we could call
 e.g. dissipation_add_taperedKO_order_min and ignore the fact that our DG
 grid is not uniform.
*This would amount to adding  const*h^3*d_x^4 g(u)  where g(u) is a function
 that compresses or stretches u in the x-dir. g arises because DG has a
 non-uniform grid.

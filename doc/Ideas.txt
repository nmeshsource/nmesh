
Improve trouble score:
======================
1st trouble score implementation in nmesh works like this:
----------------------------------------------------------
*do a full RK step
*check for trouble
*if DG and trouble, redo entire RK step in all elements

Maybe this could work:
----------------------
*get VOLRHS r for a RK substep
*compute new substep u (e.g. u += r dt/6)
*check u for trouble
*if DG and trouble:
  undo addition of r to u (e.g. u -= r dt/6)
  recompute VOLRHS r with FV
  recompute new u (e.g. u += r dt/6)
*now add SURFRHS contribution
*check u for trouble again
*if DG and trouble:
  undo addition of r to u (e.g. u -= r dt/6)
  convert u from DG to FV
  recompute SURFRHS r with FV
  recompute new u, i.e. add surface r to u

+All is local. We never need surface communication to decide if there is
 trouble!!!
+We can take substep back locally and never need to go back for all elements.

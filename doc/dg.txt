About how to normalize normal vectors, fluxes, and eigenvalues
==============================================================

For GRHD, GHG, C3GH and FOCCZ4 we have equations of the form
d_t u + A^i d_i u = s
to solve. For this we need to compute the eigenvalues of A = n_i A^i as well
"fluxes" A u = n_i A^i u. In all of these expressions there is an n_i. nmesh
has the function node_normal_at_ijk that calculates a n_i normalized with
respect to the flat metric. I think this is the n_i we need to use with the
DG method used by nmesh when we calculate n_i A^i. However, I do remember
that Liwei also makes his own normal vector s_i that is normalized with
respect to the physical metric \gamma_{ij}. But for fluxes and eigenvalues
Liwei is currently using n_i. Liwei says that he also tried with s_i
together with the other form of DG which is also self consistent, but where
everything is normalized with \gamma_{ij}. Liwei: "In the end, these two
forms performed the same, at least for the tests I have done."
